Speech and Audio Processing (SAP)

Speech and audio, from the physics of a sound to the models that understand it.

Instructor: Neeraj Kumar Sharma, IIT Guwahati

Modules

  • 01

    Seeing sound: the spectrogram

    STFT, windows and resolution; reading harmonics, formants and noise in real speech.

  • 02

    The sounds of language

    Phonemes, minimal pairs and allophones; how speech sounds are classified, and the IPA.

  • 03

    A buzz and a tube: the source–filter model

    How speech is made: a vocal-fold source shaped by the resonances of the vocal tract.

    A buzz and a tube · Vocal tract, live

  • 04

    Guessing the next sample: correlation, prediction and AR models

    Correlation, linear prediction and AR models, from sunspots to speech.

  • 05

    Linear prediction for speech

    The all-pole vocal tract: formants, LPC envelopes, analysis and resynthesis.

  • 06

    Speech codecs

    Squeezing speech into bits: quantisation, μ-law, ADPCM and the LPC vocoder.
